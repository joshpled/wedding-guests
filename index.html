Two things worth knowing

Stay seated after the ceremony. The all-guest photo only works if nobody moves. It takes ten minutes and then the bar opens.

Dinner is stations, not plated. Nothing is brought to you and nothing runs out — go up whenever you are hungry.

What to wear

Be yourself and enjoy yourself.

Coat and tie or gorgeous gown, whatever fits for you. Think Met Gala or Oscars or Tony night.

It is Charleston in September — warm and humid outside, properly air conditioned inside. The ceremony and reception are both indoors.
